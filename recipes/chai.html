Chai

This recipe shows you how to prepare a simple cup of tea

All you need are 5 simple ingredients.

Let's begin.

Ingredients:
  • Water
  • Milk
  • Tea
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom (Optional)
Steps:
  1. Take a small pot, and add 1/2 cup water, and 1/2 cup milk (along with an open pod of cardamom).
  2. Heat pot until the liquid is hot, but not boiling.
  3. Add a spoonful of tea, and a spoonful of sugar.
  4. Let it come to a boil on medium heat.
  5. Once boiled, cook for 5 more mins.
  6. Serve in a cup of tea using a strainer
