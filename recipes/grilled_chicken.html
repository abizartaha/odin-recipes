Grilled Chicken

This recipe will show you how to cook perfectly juicy grilled chicken.

All you need are 5 simple ingredients.

Let's begin.

Ingredients:
  • Chicken Breast (Cut in Half)
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil
  • Butter
Steps:
  1. Season one half of a chicken breast with salt and pepper on both sides.
  2. Put oil in a stainless steel pan and heat until lightly smoking.
  3. Make sure the flame is set to high, and put your chicken breast in the pan facing away from you.
  4. Cook for 5 mins until golden brown.
  5. Flip and continue cooking the other side.
  6. Take a thermometer and check internal temperature. Once it hits 145F, remove chicken from pan.
  7. Let chicken rest for 5 mins to let the juices redistribute.
  8. Slice and serve on a plate with veggies/mash potatoes.
