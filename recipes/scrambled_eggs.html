Scrambled Eggs

This recipe will show you how make scrambled eggs, easy peasy.

All you need are X simple ingredients.

Let's begin.

Ingredients:
  • Eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil
  • Butter
Steps:
  1. Crack two eggs in a bowl.
  2. Add a pinch of salt, and whisk thoroughly.
  3. Take a non-stick pan, set it on low heat, and add a tablespoon of oil.
  4. Take a tablespoon of butter, and melt it in the pan.
  5. Pour your eggs in the pan, and start stirring constantly.
  6. Once lumps begin to form, take the pan off the heat all the while continuously stirring.
  7. Once eggs are fully mixed and all lumps are gone, put it back on the pan. Keep stirring.
  8. Repeat this process until your desired consisteny is reached.
  9. Plate your eggs. Sprinkle black pepper on top,and serve with some warm toasted bread.
